# Imported catalogue entries and their artists

Linking pass of 8 October 2026 over the 1,224 entries imported from the old website into the 28 past-exhibition catalogues in Sanity. An eight-group classification with a skeptic per group decided, for every entry, who made it and whether the import had shifted its fields.

## Outcome

| Entries | Count | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Already linked to an artist | 123 | Left as they were |
| Artist's name was in the title field | 143 | Linked; name moved to maker, dates to maker dates, the real title, medium and origin restored from the shifted fields |
| Works in a solo exhibition with no maker written | 163 | Linked to the exhibition's artist |
| Maker name present but no artist in the inventory | 49 | Linked once the maker existed |
| Anonymous objects (antiquities, ritual objects, textiles) | 495 | Nothing to link |
| Empty records (image only) | 227 | Nothing to link; the images remain on the exhibition pages |
| Unsure | 12 | Listed below for a decision |
| Rejected by the skeptic | 12 | Listed below; left unlinked |

Entries linked in this pass: 355. Makers created in the inventory: 130 (each carries a note naming the exhibitions it came from; check the spelling and name order, merge any duplicate, switch off "Artist page" where a page is not wanted).

Leave unlinked. If linked by hand, the lossless values are: maker 'Attributed to Kalu Ram', title 'The Cosmic Egg that references the Lingam but also the singular atom from which all creation evolved', medium 'Gouache on paper', originAndDate 'Rajasthan, India'.
